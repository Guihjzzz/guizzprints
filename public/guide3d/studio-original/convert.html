Minecraft schematic converter

Convert Minecraft schematics, 3D models & worlds online

Pick a conversion below to move builds between Litematica, WorldEdit (Sponge), MCEdit, structure block, and Bedrock formats, voxelize a 3D model into blocks, turn a schematic into a playable .mcworld or Java world, or export it as a Hytale prefab. Every converter runs in your browser, previews the build in 3D, and exports without an account.

Schematic & world formats

Re-encode an existing build between the schematic, structure, and world formats used by Java, Bedrock, and Hytale.

.schem

Litematica to Sponge schematic

Move a build from the Litematica mod into tools that support the Sponge schematic format. The converter reads the regions and block palette from your Litematic file and prepares a .schem download in your browser.
